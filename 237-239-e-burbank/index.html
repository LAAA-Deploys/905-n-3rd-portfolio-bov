Buyer Profile & Transaction Strategy
Who Is Most Likely to Buy, and How We Create Competition

Market Context

Seven Burbank apartment sales of five to eleven units closed between September 2025 and July 2026 at $216,667 to $400,000 per unit. 136 N Pass Avenue, five units built in 1941, closed at $1.61M in May 2026, $322,000 per unit, a 5.23% cap on its own income. 606 N 6th Street, eight units, closed at $2.12M, a 5.89% cap, and 1608 Grismer Avenue, eight two bedroom units, at $2.4M, a 6.75% cap.

Thirty days before each of those sales closed, the 5-year Treasury ran 3.6% to 4.3%. On 09/18/2026 it stood at 4.86% (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, FRED series DGS5).

A buyer today pays more for new money than the buyers who set those comps. The assumable 3.95% loan closes most of that gap for this building, which is why it carries a tighter yield than the larger building next door.

Positioning Thesis

The right buyer wants a small, simple building with financing already in place: six identical one bedrooms, a 3.95% loan for three more years and a corner lot. That buyer should be qualified early on Chase's assumption process, because the assumed amount and the cash to close decide whether an offer holds.

Likely Buyer Profiles

Transaction Strategy

01

Price Each Building on Today's Rates

The Burbank sales in this analysis priced when the 5-year Treasury ran 3.6% to 4.4%; it stood at 4.86% on 09/18/2026. Each building is priced on its own income at a yield today's buyer can finance, so the offering draws offers in the first weeks rather than sitting while the market catches up to the ask.

02

Market the Corner as One Offering

The two parcels are one 0.83 acre corner at N 3rd Street and E Burbank Boulevard. We recommend offering them together with a separate price for each building, and taking offers on either or both, so each entity closes its own sale and the buyer who wants the whole corner can buy it.

The LAAA Team of Marcus & Millichap is pleased to present 237-239 E Burbank Boulevard, six one bedroom apartments on the corner of N 3rd Street and E Burbank Boulevard in Downtown Burbank. The wood frame buildings date to 1941 and total 4,000 square feet on a 9,769 square foot lot, with garages at the rear. The property is covered by AB 1482 and Burbank's Tenant Protection Ordinance.

The investment case is the financing. The seller's Chase loan is fixed at 3.95% through 11/2029 and is assumable, subject to Chase re-sizing it at current rates. Rents sit about 6.9% under a $2,150 market rent.

The lot adjoins 905 N 3rd Street, which is offered alongside it, and together they form a 0.83 acre corner. The offering fits buyers who want financing already in place, 1031 buyers, and the buyer of the building next door.

Location Overview
Downtown Burbank, at N 3rd Street and E Burbank Boulevard

The property sits at N 3rd Street and E Burbank Boulevard on the northwest edge of Downtown Burbank. Burbank Town Center is 0.35 miles southeast and McCambridge Park 0.5 miles northwest, and Walk Score rates the block 91 for its access to daily errands.

The Downtown Burbank Metrolink station is 0.8 miles southeast, and the NoHo to Pasadena bus rapid transit line stops on Olive Avenue about 0.6 miles away. Hollywood Burbank Airport is 2.0 miles west. Walt Disney Studios is 2.2 miles south, Providence Saint Joseph Medical Center 2.3 miles south and Warner Bros. Studios 2.9 miles southwest.

ZIP 91502 is 90% renter-occupied, with a median household income of $52,473 and a median gross rent of $1,916. The city's draft Downtown TOD Specific Plan, dated 06/23/2026, would raise this block to 87 units per acre, with hearings targeted for late fall 2026.

Financial Analysis

Pages reproduced from the supporting Marcus & Millichap financial model as printed. Owner-reported figures are unaudited. A buyer should verify all income and expenses in due diligence.

The recommended list price is $1,675,000, $279,167 per unit and $419 per SF, with an opinion of value of $1,625,000 to $1,725,000. Current scheduled rent of $144,787 less a 3% vacancy reserve gives $140,444 of effective gross income. Operating expenses of $49,230, with taxes reassessed at the list price, leave net operating income of $91,213: a 5.45% cap rate and an 11.57 gross rent multiplier. At a $2,150 market rent on all six units the net operating income is $100,440, a 6.00% cap rate.

The price sits near 136 N Pass Avenue's 5.23% cap, the closest match in age and size, and inside the comparable range on price per unit. The assumable loan is what carries the yield: a buyer who assumes it earns a positive return from the first year at this price.
